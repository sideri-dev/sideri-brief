Balmuda closes the books on its Phone line for good on September 30.

Balmuda's own notice sets a hard stop for what's left of the Phone: support, repairs, the companion app download and accessory sales all end September 30, 2026 — roughly two years after retail sales themselves stopped in September 2024. It's a clean look at how a premium Japanese hardware brand actually retires a product it no longer sells, rather than letting it fade quietly.

Mbed TLS's own vulnerability count is far worse than the widely cited figure.

Mbed TLS's own security-advisories page lists at least 18 — likely closer to 27–30 — CVE-2026 entries against its 4.1.0/4.2.0 line, well above the "five" figure that has circulated as the headline number for this release pair. Matters for anyone treating that older, smaller count as a current security baseline for embedded TLS.

KRUPS debuts in Japan on a compact-footprint story.

A Japan crowdfunding ring sold "no subscription, ever" for ¥30 million in a day.

AIVELA's Ring Pro Makuake campaign pulled in ¥30 million in its first 24 hours against a ¥500,000 target — more than 1,400 backers on day one — selling permanent full-feature access with no monthly fee, explicitly pitched against what most smart rings charge. Fresh, real-money evidence that a one-time-purchase pitch can outsell a subscription model even through crowdfunding.

Yesterday's deep-dive

GoPro's "subscriber price" on its new interchangeable-lens MISSION 1 PRO ILS camera turned out not to be the lock-in story it first looked like. The $100 discount is redeemable only at annual renewal of an already-continuous subscription, was set three weeks before GoPro's board authorized a strategic sale process, excludes the ILS camera itself from a separate broader discount, and gates no on-camera capability — only cloud storage and post-production software. The mechanic is financially small: the subscription line added $8.6 million in gross profit against a $113 million hardware loss, and the widely reported "record" 69% attach rate is largely an artifact of a 51.7% drop in units shipped. GoPro ran this same subscriber-price mechanic in Japan, abolished it in 2023 to trade-press praise as a genuine price cut, and still doesn't localize the subscription into the Japanese market today.
