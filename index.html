Yamaha's EAD50 just won Japan's Good Design grand prize

It's a single sensor clipped to a bass drum that picks up an entire drum kit at broadcast quality, turning a live performance into something mixable and streamable with no sound engineer involved. This is the first year the award ran under a new judging axis — "design that serves a vision" rather than pure form — and a device whose entire value proposition is "capture replaces a production crew" won the top prize under it, out of 1,484 winners chosen from 5,111 entries. Confirmed independently by the award organization's own release and by trade outlet MONOist.

A Tokyo exhibition rebuilt a vanished building at full scale using nothing but a novelist's written descriptions of it

21_21 DESIGN SIGHT's "方丈記に学ぶ" (open now through January 11, 2027) takes the 13th-century hermit's-hut essay Hōjōki as its spine. Yesterday's deep dive

Yesterday we took apart a Meta Reality Labs research paper modeling the power draw of an always-on wearable AI device and found less than its headline claimed. The paper gives no absolute power figures anywhere — every number is deliberately obfuscated or rounded "to protect confidential information" — and its "no single component dominates power" finding turns out to be a 37% plurality held by one wireless radio interface. It also contradicts itself on the sign of its own central result, stating in one place that running computation on-device uses 16% less power and in another that it uses 20% more. Its claim to be the first such model also overlooks prior published work that reached the same structural finding by actual hardware measurement five years earlier. The most useful thing we found: a separate, measured study on our own exact board class (a Raspberry Pi 5) found the board itself accounts for 85–93% of total power draw — the opposite emphasis from Meta's simulated numbers on very different hardware. The general method — model the whole system before optimizing any one part — still transfers to our own planning; Meta's specific conclusion about where the power goes does not.
