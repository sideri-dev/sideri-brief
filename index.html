Yesterday's deep-dive

A deep-dive into a Singapore wireless-registration expiry headline found the original story's premise wrong: the expiring certificate belongs to a compliance agent, not to Raspberry Pi, and under Singapore's own rules a renewal wouldn't even be a usable credential for a different seller — every supplier has to register independently regardless of who registered first. What expiry actually blocks is new inventory sales, not equipment already in the field, and renewal for this specific board is free and available in a two-month window already open. Thailand's parallel filing has no expiry at all, only revocation for cause. One thread stays open: whether a direct-to-consumer seller shipping into Singapore even triggers this registration regime in the first place.
